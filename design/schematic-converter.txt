BENCH SUPPLY - CONVERTER BOARD, ONE PER CHANNEL
LT8705 SYNCHRONOUS 4-SWITCH BUCK-BOOST, 2-60 V / 3 A to 150 W, 200 kHz
Two of these, identical, isolated from each other. See docs/power-chain.md
Fed from schematic-rectifier.txt - one rectifier board per converter board.
===============================================================================

  28 VAC in from its OWN toroid  ->  ~29-44 V rail  ->  2-60 V out, floating
  Isolated from the other channel by the transformers, so the two outputs
  stack for +/-60 V or 120 V in series. NOTHING on this board may share a
  ground with the control side except through the optocouplers.

  PIN NAMES below are functional. CHECK THEM AND THE PIN NUMBERS against the
  LT8705 datasheet before drawing - this document is the design intent, not a
  transcription.


1. INPUT - FROM THE RECTIFIER BOARD
-----------------------------------

  +RAIL (29-44 V) and RTN arrive from schematic-rectifier.txt, through that
  board's 10 A fast fuse. Heavy pair, but its inductance does not matter:
  this is a 6 A dc feed with 120 Hz ripple, not a switching loop.

  ** Cin IS WHAT MAKES THAT TRUE, AND IT LIVES HERE. **

      +RAIL o---+-------+-------+-------+----> to QA drain, SHORT
                |       |       |       |
               10u     10u     10u     10u     4 x 10 uF / 100 V X7R
               ===     ===     ===     ===     right at the FETs
                |       |       |       |
      RTN   o---+-------+-------+-------+----> to QB source, SHORT

  Cin takes all the di/dt. Worst case is BUCK mode, where the input current
  is pulsed rather than continuous:

      Vout  2 V, D 0.068 -> 0.76 A rms in Cin,  26 mV ripple
      Vout 12 V, D 0.410 -> 1.48 A rms in Cin, 154 mV ripple
      Vout 24 V, D 0.819 -> 1.15 A rms in Cin, 307 mV ripple

  In boost mode the input current is the inductor current and is continuous,
  so Cin barely notices. ~40 uF of 100 V ceramic covers both.


2. POWER STAGE
--------------

         +RAIL                                                    VOUT
           o                                                       o
           |                                                       |
          ===  Cin                                                ===  Cout
          10u x4                                                  bulk polymer
          100 V                                                   100 V + 22u
           |          QA            L1            QD                |  ceramics
           +----------+-+---+-------UUUUU-------+-+---+-------------+
           |          | D |         41.5 uH     | D |               |
           |        G-+-+-+  SW1          SW2   +-+-+-G             |
           |            S |                       | S               |
           |              |                       |                 |
           |          QB  |                   QC  |                 |
           |          +-+-+                   +-+-+                 |
           |          | D |                   | D |                 |
           |        G-+-+-+                   +-+-+-G               |
           |            S |                     | S                 |
           +--------------+---------------------+-------------------+
           |                                                        |
        Rsen_IN                                                  Rsen_OUT
        5 mOhm                                                   15 mOhm
        2512, 1 W                                                2512, 1/2 W
           |                                                        |
           o RTN                                                    o RTN

  QA..QD : 100 V, 20-30 mOhm, Qg <= 50 nC.  ONE part number, four positions,
           but NOT the same duty - see docs/power-chain.md#the-four-switches.
           QA carries the full 6.02 A continuously in boost and does no
           switching; QC and QD take all the switching loss.
           All four TABS ARE AT DIFFERENT POTENTIALS: individual insulators
           on the shared heatsink.

  L1     : Magnetics 0077083A7, Kool Mu 0254, 60u, hand wound.
           24 turns of 3 x AWG18 twisted. Wind 25 and measure - AL is +/-8%,
           which is two turns of spread on its own.
           Wound coil ~OD 43, HT 19 mm. Custom footprint: two pads plus a
           keepout. Do NOT pot it until it measures right.

  Rsen_IN  5 mOhm -> 50 mV at 7.4 A.  This is the INPUT CURRENT LIMIT and it
           is what stops the transformer being overdrawn. It makes the 150 W
           envelope a hardware property rather than a firmware promise.
  Rsen_OUT 15 mOhm -> 50 mV at 3 A.  The user-set CC limit.
           KELVIN both: sense traces from the pad ends, not from the power path.

  EXTVCC matters here: Qg x 4 x 200 kHz is 24-40 mA out of the internal
  regulator at 30-50 nC, and more if a fatter FET is substituted. Feeding
  EXTVCC externally takes the regulator out of the argument.

  The four SRVO_* pins indicate which loop is active - bring them out to the
  ADC so the Pico can display CV / CC / input-limited.


4. SETPOINT: THE SWITCHED CONDUCTANCE DIVIDER
---------------------------------------------

  Vout = Vref * (1 + Rtop * G),  Vref = 1.207 V (FBOUT),  Rtop = 48.7k
  Vout is LINEAR in bottom-leg conductance, so binary-weighted parallel
  resistors give UNIFORM voltage steps. 10 bits = 57 mV over 2-60 V.

      VOUT o---[ Rtop 48.7k 0.1% ]---+---o FBOUT
                                     |
                      +--------------+----------+---- ... ----+
                      |              |          |             |
                   Rfix 74.1k      R9 2.02k   R8 4.05k   ... R0 1037k
                   (no switch)       |          |             |
                                    SW9        SW8           SW0
                                     |          |             |
                                     +----------+---- ... ----+---o RTN

    bit  conductance    resistor        bit  conductance   resistor
     9    493.84 uS      2.02 k          4     15.43 uS     64.80 k
     8    246.92 uS      4.05 k          3      7.72 uS    129.60 k
     7    123.46 uS      8.10 k          2      3.86 uS    259.19 k
     6     61.73 uS     16.20 k          1      1.93 uS    518.39 k
     5     30.87 uS     32.40 k          0      0.96 uS   1036.77 k
    fixed  13.49 uS     74.10 k  -> 2.0 V with every switch OPEN

  ** THE FIXED BRANCH SETS THE MINIMUM AND THE SWITCHES ONLY ADD. **
  Switches open - unpowered, in reset, mid-reflash - is 2 V out, not 60.
  Wire it the other way round and every one of those states commands full
  output. This is structural and must not be "simplified" later.

  SW0..SW9 : two ADG714-class octal SPST in series on one SPI chain.
             ~2.5 Ohm on-resistance: 0.25% error at 60 V, where Rbot is 1k.
             A CD4051 at ~100 Ohm would cost 10% there.
             VERIFY the power-up state is all-switches-OPEN.
  Tolerance: 1% throughout, 0.1% on Rtop and bits 7-9. The 1 M LSB's
             tolerance is worth less than one step.


5. ISOLATED CONTROL - EVERYTHING CROSSES OPTICALLY
--------------------------------------------------

  Pico side                 | barrier |            THIS BOARD's ground
  ------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------
  SCK   --[330R]--|>|-------|    |    |-------[10k pull-up]--> ADG714 SCLK
  MOSI  --[330R]--|>|-------|    |    |-------[10k pull-up]--> ADG714 SDI
  CS_SW --[330R]--|>|-------|    |    |-------[10k pull-up]--> ADG714 SYNC
  CS_AD --[330R]--|>|-------|    |    |-------[10k pull-up]--> ADC CS
  MISO  <---[10k]---|<|-----|    |    |<--[330R]-- ADC DOUT / ADG714 SDO
  ------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------

  FIVE optos. Four out with their LEDs on the Pico side, ONE back with its
  LED on THIS side. Nothing electrical bridges the gap - these two channels
  get stacked in series, so a shared ground would defeat the transformers.

  SPI, not I2C: every opto is unidirectional and I2C's SDA is not.
  Clock at a few kHz. A 10k pull-up and a phototransistor make edges tens of
  microseconds long; 10 bits then takes single-digit milliseconds, which is
  invisible to someone turning a knob.

  Switch-state readback is free: the ADG714 chain's SDO returns the PREVIOUS
  word on the MISO opto already there for the ADC. Compare it with what was
  sent. On a board that can put 60 V out, a stuck bit is a silently wrong
  output voltage.

  ADC : 12-bit SPI, 4 channels -> Vout divider, Iout (from INA on Rsen_OUT),
        Iin (INA on Rsen_IN), and the SRVO_* loop-status pins.
  INA : high-side, >=80 V common mode (INA293-class). 20 mOhm x gain 50 gives
        3.0 V full scale. A 26 V part will NOT do - the output is the common
        mode and it goes to 60.


6. LOCAL BIAS - AND WHAT IT MUST NOT BE
---------------------------------------

  +RAIL (29-44 V) --> small 60 V buck --> 12 V --> EXTVCC
                                            |
                                            +---> 5 V LDO --> ADG714, ADC,
                                                              INA, opto
                                                              pull-ups

  ** NOT from the control board's 5 V module. ** Feeding this side from the
  control rail wires the two channels together and undoes the isolation the
  second transformer was bought for. Each channel powers its own logic from
  its own rail, which is isolated by construction.


7. OUTPUT
---------

        VOUT o----+--------------------------o + TERMINAL
                  |
                 D_rev   across the output, cathode to +, anode to -
                  |      REVERSE-BIASED in normal use, carries nothing.
        RTN  o----+--------------------------o - TERMINAL
                         Only conducts if the other channel drives this one
                         backwards while it is off, current-limited or
                         shorted. Rate it for the full output current.

  NOTHING IN SERIES WITH THE OUTPUT. A 0.5 V drop is a quarter of the 2 V
  floor, and 1.5 W of heat the load never sees. The same argument is why the
  converter is synchronous.


8. WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT ON THIS BOARD
-----------------------------------------

  * No microcontroller. The isolated side holds no firmware - only switches,
    an ADC and a sense amp, doing what they are told. "Switches open = 2 V"
    is then structural and cannot be undone by a hung program.
  * No mains. The transformer primary, its fuse and the inlet live on the
    back wall behind their own shield.
  * No CC error amplifier. The LT8705 carries that loop itself.
  * No fan drive, no display, no 1-Wire. Control board.
